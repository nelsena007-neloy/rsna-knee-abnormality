# Data Pipeline Specification

## 1. MRI DICOM Ingestion & Normalization

1. **Pixel Array Standardization:**
   - Rescaling via Rescale Slope & Intercept: $HU = m \cdot x + b$.
   - Z-score intensity normalization with dynamic 1st and 99th percentile contrast clipping.
   - Spatial resampling to standardized $0.4 \text{ mm} \times 0.4 \text{ mm}$ in-plane pixel spacing.

2. **Sequence-Aware Classification:**
   - Sagittal Proton Density Fat-Suppressed (PD-FS): Crucial for ACL tears, PCL integrity, and cartilage fibrillations.
   - Coronal T2-TSE: Gold standard for MCL/LCL sprains, medial/lateral meniscal tears, and tibial plateau contusions.
   - Axial PD: Primary view for patellofemoral osteoarthritis, trochlear dysplasia, and Baker's popliteal cysts.

3. **2.5D Adaptive Windowing:**
   - 3-slice tri-channel stacking: $[S_{i-1}, S_i, S_{i+1}]$ fed into pre-trained visual encoders to capture spatial continuity across the slice thickness axis.
